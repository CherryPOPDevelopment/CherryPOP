General

Working together

Questions that apply to all services.

Do you work with clients outside the UK?

Yes — I work with clients worldwide. All communication is remote via video call and messaging.

How does payment work?

Projects are split into two payments: 50% upfront to begin work, and 50% on completion before final handover. No payment is taken until we've agreed on scope and you're comfortable to proceed. I accept bank transfer and major cards.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes — I'm happy to sign a mutual NDA before any sensitive project details are shared. Just mention it when you reach out and I'll send one over straight away.

Who owns the code and assets after the project?

You own 100% of everything once the project is complete and final payment is made — all code, designs, and assets are fully yours. I retain no rights to your project.

How do revisions work?

Revisions are included in every tier. Starter plans include 2 rounds, Professional includes 3, and Enterprise includes unlimited rounds. A revision round covers a batch of changes reviewed together — I always show you a staging preview before anything goes live.

What if my project doesn't fit a standard tier?

No problem — use the Contact for Custom button on any service page or visit the Contact page. I'll put together a bespoke quote at no obligation.

Can you work to a tight deadline?

Rush delivery is available. If you have a tight deadline, mention it in your quote request and I'll let you know if it's achievable. Rush projects may carry a small premium depending on scope.

What do you need from me to get started?

A brief describing your goals, any brand assets (logo, colours), and access to your domain if you have one. The more detail upfront, the smoother the project runs.

What if I need to cancel?

If you cancel before work begins, your deposit is fully refundable. Once work has started, the deposit covers work completed to that point. I always confirm scope before any payment, so we're both aligned from day one.

How does buying a package online work?

Click Buy This Package on any service page, fill in your details and project description. I'll review your order and reach out within 24 hours to discuss details — no payment is taken until we're aligned.

How are your prices benchmarked?

Prices reflect typical beginner–intermediate freelance market rates. I regularly reference Fiverr's developer cost guides to ensure tiers stay competitive and fair.

Websites

Website development

View website packages →

Is hosting included in the price?

Hosting is not included — I'll recommend the best option for your project (typically a VPS, Cloudflare Pages, or Vercel) and help you set it up. Costs are usually $5–$20/month depending on traffic.

Will my site be mobile-friendly?

Every site I build is fully responsive and tested across mobile, tablet, and desktop. Mobile-first design is standard practice on all tiers.

Do you include SEO?

Yes — all tiers include on-page SEO basics: proper heading structure, meta tags, Open Graph tags, and a sitemap. Professional and Enterprise tiers also include keyword research and performance optimisation.

Can I update the site myself after launch?

Absolutely. Professional and Enterprise tiers include a CMS so you can edit content without touching code.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes — monthly maintenance packages are available from $79/month covering updates, backups, and minor changes.

How are website prices benchmarked?

According to Fiverr's 2026 Software Developer Cost Guide, freelance website builds typically range from $54 to $658+. My tiers sit within that range, structured by complexity and deliverables so you know exactly what you're getting.

Apps

App development

View app packages →

Do I need separate apps for iOS and Android?

No — I use React Native which produces a single codebase that runs natively on both platforms.

Can you publish to the App Store / Google Play?

Yes — App Store and Google Play submission is included in the Growth and Scale tiers.

What if I already have a design?

Great — I can work from Figma, Adobe XD, or any other design format, which can reduce the timeline.

Can the app connect to my existing backend?

Absolutely. If you have an existing API or database, I'll integrate the app with it rather than rebuilding from scratch.

Will the app be tested before launch?

Yes — every app goes through functional testing on both iOS and Android simulators, plus real-device testing before submission. Bug fixes discovered during testing are included in scope.

What about App Store / Google Play fees?

Developer account fees are separate — Apple charges $99/year and Google charges a one-time $25 fee. I'll guide you through the setup if you don't have accounts yet.

Can I add features after launch?

Yes — ongoing feature development is available after launch. We can scope additional work as a new project or on a retainer basis.

How are app prices benchmarked?

According to Fiverr's 2026 App Developer Cost Guide, app developer pricing ranges from $45 to over $3,400 per project depending on scope. My tiers start at entry level and scale up with complexity — no hidden costs.

Shops

E-commerce & shops

View shop packages →

Shopify or WooCommerce — which is better for me?

Shopify is great for simplicity and speed; WooCommerce gives more flexibility if you're already on WordPress. I'll recommend the right fit based on your needs.

Can you migrate my existing store?

Yes — I can migrate products, customer data, and order history from most platforms.

Will I be able to manage the store myself?

Absolutely. Every tier includes a handover session so you're confident managing products, orders, and content.

Do the prices include Shopify subscription fees?

No — Shopify's monthly fee is separate and paid directly to Shopify. I'll advise which plan you need.

Can you set up payment processing?

Yes — I'll connect your preferred payment gateway (Stripe, PayPal, Shopify Payments, etc.) and test the full checkout flow before launch.

Will the store be mobile-optimised?

Every store I build is fully responsive and tested on mobile, tablet, and desktop. With most e-commerce traffic coming from mobile, this is non-negotiable.

Can you help with shipping setup?

Yes — I can configure shipping zones, rates, and carrier integrations (e.g. USPS, UPS, Royal Mail) as part of the build.

How are shop prices benchmarked?

According to Fiverr's 2026 E-commerce Developer Cost Guide, Shopify and WooCommerce builds typically range from $50 to $1,150+. My tiers are priced accessibly within that range with clear deliverables at every level.
