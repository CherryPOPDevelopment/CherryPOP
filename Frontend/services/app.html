FAQ

Common questions

Do I need separate apps for iOS and Android?

No — I use React Native which produces a single codebase that runs natively on both platforms.

Can you publish to the App Store / Google Play?

Yes, App Store and Google Play submission is included in the Growth and Scale tiers.

What if I already have a design?

Great — I can work from Figma, Adobe XD, or any other design format, which can reduce the timeline.

Can the app connect to my existing backend?

Absolutely. If you have an existing API or database, I'll integrate the app with it rather than rebuilding from scratch.

Will the app be tested before launch?

Yes — every app goes through functional testing on both iOS and Android simulators, plus real-device testing before submission. Bug fixes discovered during testing are included in scope.

Who owns the code after the project?

You own 100% of the code and assets once final payment is made. You'll receive the full source code and any project files.

What about App Store / Google Play fees?

Developer account fees are separate — Apple charges $99/year and Google charges a one-time $25 fee. I'll guide you through the setup if you don't have accounts yet.

Can I add features after launch?

Yes — ongoing feature development is available after launch. We can scope additional work as a new project or on a retainer basis.

Do you sign NDAs?

Absolutely — I'm happy to sign a mutual NDA before any sensitive details are shared. Just mention it when you reach out.

How does payment work?

Projects are split 50% upfront and 50% on completion. No payment is taken until we've agreed on scope and you're happy to proceed.

How does buying a package online work?

Click Buy This Package on any tier, fill in your name, email, and a description of your app idea — including any specific screens or features. I'll review your order and reach out within 24 hours to confirm scope before any payment is taken.

What if my app idea is more complex than a standard tier?

Use Contact for Custom or visit the Contact page. I'll put together a tailored quote at no obligation — complex projects are my speciality.

How are your prices benchmarked?

Prices reflect current freelance market rates for mobile development. According to Fiverr's 2026 App Developer Cost Guide, app developer pricing ranges from $45 to over $3,400 per project depending on scope. My tiers start at the entry level and scale up with complexity — no hidden costs.
