Shopify or WooCommerce — which is better for me?

Shopify is great for simplicity and speed; WooCommerce gives more flexibility if you're already on WordPress. I'll recommend the right fit based on your needs.

Can you migrate my existing store?

Yes — I can migrate products, customer data, and order history from most platforms.

Will I be able to manage the store myself?

Absolutely. Every tier includes a handover session so you're confident managing products, orders, and content.

Do the prices include Shopify subscription fees?

No — Shopify's monthly fee is separate and paid directly to Shopify. I'll advise which plan you need.
