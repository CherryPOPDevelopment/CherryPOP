FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with clients outside the UK?

Yes — I work with clients worldwide. All communication is remote via video call and messaging.

What do you need from me to get started?

A brief describing your goals, any brand assets (logo, colours), and access to your domain if you have one.

Can I update the site myself after launch?

Absolutely. Professional and Enterprise tiers include a CMS so you can edit content without touching code.

Is hosting included in the price?

Hosting is not included — I'll recommend the best option for your project (typically a VPS, Cloudflare Pages, or Vercel) and help you set it up. Costs are usually $5–$20/month depending on traffic.

Will my site be mobile-friendly?

Every site I build is fully responsive and tested across mobile, tablet, and desktop. Mobile-first design is standard practice on all tiers.

Do you include SEO?

Yes — all tiers include on-page SEO basics: proper heading structure, meta tags, Open Graph tags, and a sitemap. Professional and Enterprise tiers also include keyword research and performance optimisation.

How do revisions work?

Each tier includes a set number of revision rounds (2 for Starter, 3 for Professional, unlimited for Enterprise). A revision round covers a batch of changes — I'll always show you a staging preview before anything goes live.

Who owns the code and design after launch?

You do. Once the project is complete and final payment is made, all code and design assets are fully yours. I retain no rights to your project.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes — I'm happy to sign a mutual NDA before any sensitive project details are shared. Just mention it when you reach out.

How does payment work?

Projects are split into two payments: 50% upfront to begin work, and 50% on completion before the final handover. No payment is taken until we've agreed on scope and you're comfortable to proceed.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes — monthly maintenance packages are available from $79/month covering updates, backups, and minor changes.

How does buying a package online work?

Click Buy This Package on any tier, fill in your name, email, and a description of what you need — including any customisation. I'll review your order and reach out within 24 hours to discuss details before any payment is taken.

What if my project doesn't fit a standard tier?

Use the Contact for Custom button or visit the Contact page to describe your needs. I'll put together a bespoke quote at no obligation.

How are your prices benchmarked?

Prices are set to reflect typical beginner–intermediate freelance market rates. According to Fiverr's 2026 Software Developer Cost Guide, freelance website builds typically range from $54 to $658+. My tiers sit within that range, structured by complexity and deliverables so you know exactly what you're getting.
