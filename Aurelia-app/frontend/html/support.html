Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?
To create an account, click on the "Register" button on the login page. You'll need to provide:
  • First Name and Last Name
  • Username (unique identifier)
  • Email address
  • Password (minimum 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, and number)
  • Birthday (must be at least 13 years old)

Important: After registration, you'll receive a verification email with a 6-digit code. You must verify your email before you can log in. Check your inbox (and spam folder) for the verification code.

You'll also need to complete the reCAPTCHA verification to prevent automated account creation.

How do I add a contact?
To add a contact:
  1. Click the "+ Add Contact" button in the sidebar
  2. Fill in the contact's information (name and email are required)
  3. Optionally add phone, address, category, and notes
  4. Choose what information you want to share with friends (if any)
  5. Click "Save Contact"
Your contact will be saved and appear in your contacts list.
Can I import contacts from a file?
Yes! Aurelia Contacts supports importing contacts from:
  • CSV files: Comma-separated values format (e.g., exported from Excel or Google Contacts)
  • vCard files (.vcf): Standard contact format used by many email clients and address books

To import contacts:

  1. Go to the Contacts section
  2. Look for the "Import" button or option
  3. Select your CSV or .vcf file
  4. Review the imported contacts and make any necessary adjustments
  5. Save the imported contacts

You can also export your contacts for backup or to use in other applications.

What are contact cards and how do I share them?
Contact cards are contacts that you've chosen to share with your friends. When adding or editing a contact, you can select what information to share:
  • Nothing (Private): Contact is only visible to you
  • Email: Share only the email address
  • Phone: Share only the phone number
  • Email & Phone: Share both email and phone
  • All info: Share all contact information
Only your friends can see shared contact cards on your profile.
How do I add friends?
To add friends:
  1. Click "+ Find Friends" in the sidebar or navigate to the Friends section
  2. Browse all users in the system
  3. Click "Add Friend" on a user's profile
  4. They will receive a friend request
  5. Once accepted, you'll be friends and can see each other's shared contact cards
You can also search for specific users by name or username.
How do I update my profile information?
To update your profile:
  1. Click "Profile" in the navigation bar
  2. Edit your information in the Profile tab (name, bio, phone, profile picture)
  3. Click "Save Profile" to save your changes

Note: Your birthday cannot be changed once it's set. If you need to correct your birthday, please contact support.

Your profile information is saved permanently and will persist across sessions.

How do I upload or change my profile picture?
To upload or change your profile picture:
  1. Go to your Profile page
  2. Click the "Upload Photo" button on your profile picture area
  3. Select an image from your computer, or drag and drop an image onto the upload area
  4. Use the cropping tool to adjust your image:
    • Drag to reposition the image
    • Use rotate buttons to rotate left or right
    • Use flip buttons to flip horizontally or vertically
    • Use zoom controls to zoom in or out
    • Resize the crop box if needed
  5. Click "Crop & Save" when you're happy with the result
  6. Click "Save Profile" to permanently save your profile picture

Requirements: Images can be JPG, PNG, or GIF format, with a maximum size of 15MB. Your image will be automatically cropped to a square (1:1 ratio) at high quality (1024x1024px).

Can I change my birthday?

No, you cannot change your birthday once it's been set. This is a security measure to prevent account manipulation.

If your birthday is incorrect or you need to update it for any reason, please contact our support team:

  • Email: support@aureliacontacts.com
  • Include your username and the correct birthday in your message
  • We'll verify your identity and update it for you

Please note that birthday changes are only made in exceptional circumstances and require verification.

How do I control my privacy settings?
You can control your privacy in the Profile page under the "Privacy" tab:
  • Contact Information Sharing: Choose what information friends can see by default
  • Profile Visibility: Control if your profile is discoverable
  • Friend Requests: Set who can send you friend requests
You can also set sharing preferences for individual contacts when adding or editing them.
How do I change my password?
To change your password:
  1. Go to Profile → Settings tab
  2. Click "Change Password"
  3. Enter your current password
  4. Enter your new password
  5. Confirm the change

Make sure to use a strong, unique password for security (minimum 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, and number).

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
If you've forgotten your password:
  1. Go to the login page
  2. Click "Forgot password?" below the login form
  3. Enter your email address
  4. Check your email inbox (and spam folder) for a password reset email
  5. Click the "Reset Password" button in the email (or copy the reset link)
  6. Enter your new password twice (must match)
  7. Click "Reset Password" to save
  8. You'll be redirected to login with your new password

Note: Reset links expire after 1 hour for security. If your link has expired, request a new one.

If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support at support@aureliacontacts.com

What is email verification and why do I need it?
Email verification is a security feature that ensures you own the email address you registered with:
  • New Users: After registering, you'll receive an email with a 6-digit verification code
  • Verification Required: You must verify your email before you can log in
  • Where to Verify: You'll be automatically redirected to the verification page after registration
  • Code Expiration: Verification codes expire after 24 hours
  • One-Time Use: Once verified, you won't need to verify again for future logins

If you didn't receive the code: Check your spam folder, or contact support if you still don't see it after a few minutes.

This helps protect your account and prevents fake accounts from being created.

Where is my data stored?
All your data is stored securely in a MySQL database:
  • User accounts: Stored in the users table
  • Contacts: Stored in the contacts table
  • Friendships: Stored in the friendships table
Your data is private and only accessible to you and users you've explicitly shared information with. All data persists across sessions and is backed up in the database.
Can I delete my account?
Yes, you can delete your account:
  1. Go to Profile → Settings tab
  2. Scroll to "Account Management"
  3. Click "Delete Account"
  4. Enter your password to confirm
Warning: This will permanently delete all your contacts, friendships, and account data. This action cannot be undone.
I'm having trouble logging in. What should I do?
If you're having login issues:
  • New Users: Make sure you've verified your email address. Check your inbox for a verification code if you haven't verified yet.
  • Make sure you're using the correct email and password
  • Check if Caps Lock is enabled
  • Try the "Forgot Password" feature to reset your password
  • Complete the reCAPTCHA verification if prompted (this prevents automated login attempts)
  • Clear your browser cache and cookies
  • Make sure JavaScript is enabled in your browser
  • Try using a different browser or device
If problems persist, contact support at support@aureliacontacts.com
What is reCAPTCHA and why do I see it?
reCAPTCHA is a security feature that helps protect Aurelia Contacts from automated bots and spam:
  • When you see it: During registration and login attempts
  • What it does: Verifies that you're a real person, not an automated program
  • How to complete: Simply check the box or follow the simple verification instructions
  • Why it's important: Prevents fake accounts, spam, and automated attacks on the system

Most users complete reCAPTCHA in just a few seconds. If you have trouble with reCAPTCHA, make sure your browser is up to date and JavaScript is enabled.

How do I switch between light and dark theme?
To change your theme:
  1. Go to your Profile page
  2. Click on the "Settings" tab
  3. Find the "Theme" section
  4. Select your preferred theme:
    • Light: Bright, clean interface (default)
    • Dark: Dark mode with reduced eye strain for nighttime use
    • System: Follows your device preference (defaults to light)

Your theme preference is saved automatically and will persist across all your sessions. The change applies instantly to the entire application.

Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you:

  • Create an account (username, email, password, name, birthday)
  • Verify your email address (6-digit verification code)
  • Update your profile (bio, phone, birthday, profile picture)
  • Upload profile pictures (stored securely and cropped to standard format)
  • Add contacts (name, email, phone, address, notes)
  • Import contacts from CSV or vCard files
  • Connect with friends (friend requests and relationships)
  • Use password reset functionality (temporary reset tokens)
  • Select theme preferences (stored locally and in your account settings)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Verify your email address to ensure account security
  • Send password reset links when requested
  • Send welcome emails and verification codes
  • Process your transactions and send related information
  • Send you technical notices and support messages
  • Respond to your comments and questions
  • Facilitate friend connections and contact sharing (only with your explicit permission)
  • Remember your preferences (such as theme settings)
  • Protect against fraud, spam, and abuse through reCAPTCHA verification

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information:

  • With your consent: When you explicitly choose to share contact cards with friends
  • With friends: Only the information you've marked as "shared" in your contact preferences
  • For legal reasons: If required by law or to protect our rights

You have full control over what information you share with friends through your privacy settings.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with salt rounds
  • Email verification required for all new accounts
  • Password reset tokens expire after 1 hour
  • Verification codes expire after 24 hours
  • reCAPTCHA protection against automated attacks
  • Rate limiting to prevent abuse and DDoS attacks
  • Database connections are secured
  • JWT tokens for secure session management (7-day expiration)
  • Data is stored in a secure MySQL database
  • Input validation and sanitization to prevent injection attacks

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information through your profile
  • Update or correct your information at any time
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Control what information you share with friends
  • Unfriend users at any time

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. When you delete your account, all your data is permanently removed from our database.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
